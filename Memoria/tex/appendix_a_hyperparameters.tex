\section{BC teacher (shield-as-teacher, R2)}\label{app:hp-bc}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------

When the BC teacher is enabled (Chapter~\ref{ch:dependence}), three additional
hyperparameters control the behavioural-cloning loss added on top of the PPO
surrogate.

\begin{table}[H]
  \centering
  \caption{BC-teacher hyperparameters (\texttt{ppo\_agent.py} constructor and CLI flags).}
  \label{tab:hp-bc}
  \small
  \begin{tabular}{@{}llc@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Parameter} & \textbf{CLI flag / constructor} & \textbf{Default} \\
    \midrule
    BC loss weight, initial (\(\lambda_{\mathrm{BC}}\))
      & \texttt{--shield\_imitation\_coef} & \(0.0\) (disabled) \\
    BC loss anneal updates
      & \texttt{--shield\_imitation\_anneal\_updates} & \(150\) \\
    Clone steering only
      & \texttt{--shield\_imitation\_steer\_only} & \texttt{True} \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

The BC loss is applied only when \(\lambda_{\mathrm{BC}} > 0\).  It targets the
steering dimension of the shield-corrected action and is weighted by the shield
blending factor \(\alpha_t\):
\[
  \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{BC}}(\theta)
  = \frac{\displaystyle\sum_t \alpha_t\,
          \bigl(\tanh\bigl(\mu^{\mathrm{steer}}_\theta(s_t)\bigr)
                - a^{\star,\mathrm{steer}}_t\bigr)^2}
         {\displaystyle\sum_t \alpha_t}.
\]
The coefficient \(\lambda_{\mathrm{BC}}\) is annealed linearly to zero over
\texttt{anneal\_updates} PPO gradient updates so that the shield acts as an
early teacher but releases the policy as training matures.

\section{LLM meta-controller}\label{app:hp-meta}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------

The LLM meta-controller (Chapter~\ref{ch:dependence}) comprises three components:
an LLM-based permissiveness proposer, a deterministic safety governor that
mediates the proposal, and a tunable-shield layer that maps the scalar
permissiveness \(p \in [0,1]\) to concrete threshold values.

\subsection*{Tunable-shield weanable endpoints (\texttt{src/meta/tunable\_shield.py})}

Each weanable parameter is interpolated linearly between a \emph{strict} value
(enforced at \(p=1\)) and a \emph{permissive} value (reached at \(p=0\)):
\(\tau_i(p) = \tau_i^{\mathrm{strict}} + (1-p)(\tau_i^{\mathrm{perm}} - \tau_i^{\mathrm{strict}})\).
